Deformation Kinetics of Charged Nanoclusters via Surface Diffusion

Efstratios M. Kritikos, William A. Goddard, Paul M. Bellan

arXiv:2611.17514·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-12

Low-temperature charged nanoclusters deform, primarily via surface diffusion, when surface electrostatic stresses and surface tension stresses are not balanced. We derive an analytical model for the kinetics of this process, based on the cluster size, net charge, surface tension, local curvature, temperature, and diffusivity. The model predicts the deformation time when either surface tension or electrostatic stress dominates, leading respectively to the collapse or elongation of the cluster. Comparison with molecular dynamics simulations of ice nanoclusters with solvated electrons shows quantitative agreement with a single fitted parameter. The model can predict the deformation kinetics of charged nanoclusters in atmospheric aerosols and complex plasmas.
