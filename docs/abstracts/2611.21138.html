Orbital-dependent electronic correlations and single-band character in layered palladates

Jing-Xuan Wang, Jia-Ming Wang, Rong-Qiang He, Zhong-Yi Lu

arXiv:2611.21138·cond-mat.str-el·Published 2026-11-16

Layered palladates provide a $4d$ platform for examining electronic correlations in materials with cuprate-like structural and orbital characteristics. Using fully charge-self-consistent density functional theory plus dynamical mean-field theory, we investigate the orbital- and layer-resolved electronic structures of LaPdO$_2$, RbSr$_2$PdO$_3$, BaLaPdO$_2$Cl$_2$, La$_4$Pd$_3$O$_8$, and La$_5$Pd$_4$O$_{10}$. Under the conditions studied, all compounds remain metallic, with low-energy states dominated by the Pd-$d_{x^2-y^2}$ orbital. This orbital exhibits stronger mass renormalization than the Pd-$d_{z^2}$ orbital. LaPdO$_2$ and BaLaPdO$_2$Cl$_2$ show moderate correlations, whereas RbSr$_2$PdO$_3$ is more weakly correlated. Both the trilayer La$_4$Pd$_3$O$_8$ and quadlayer La$_5$Pd$_4$O$_{10}$ remain weakly correlated, with similar Pd-$d_{x^2-y^2}$ mass enhancements and little difference between inner- and outer-layer Pd sites. Among the structures studied, BaLaPdO$_2$Cl$_2$ is closest to a cuprate-like single-band description, whereas RbSr$_2$PdO$_3$ retains an additional electron pocket with Pd-$d_{z^2}$ character. These results establish a comparative picture of weak to moderate, orbital-dependent electronic correlations in layered palladates.
