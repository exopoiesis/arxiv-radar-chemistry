Single-electron fluorine bonding. II. Benchmarking Minnesota density functional approximations and popular ab initio methods for binding energies (BEs) and F-bond distances

Govinda Prasad Khanal, Elangannan Arunan

arXiv:2610.23472·physics.chem-ph·Published 2026-10-16

Companion paper I (Khanal and Arunan, in this issue; arXiv:2610.16629 [physics.chem-ph]) reported reference BEs and F-bond distances for two new databases SEFB-15 (DF$\cdots$CH$_3$) and CSFB-14 (DF$\cdots$NH$_3$). Here, we assess performance of popular ab initio methods (MP2, MP3, MP4(DQ), MP4(SDQ), CCSD, and QCISD) with valence- and full-electron correlation treatments, and sixteen Minnesota DFAs, including M06-2X already assessed in companion paper I. CCSD/AVDZ reproduces reference F-bond distances closely, although BEs predicted by it deviate more. Among the 16 Minnesota DFAs, M08-HX reliably reproduces both reference values for DF$\cdots$CH$_3$ and DF$\cdots$NH$_3$, comparable to M05-2X and M06-2X for DF$\cdots$NH$_3$. Selected meta-GGAs (M06-L, M11-L, and MN15-L) exhibit spurious fractional charge transfer in cationic complexes even at infinite separation, whereas hybrid Minnesota DFAs do not, except M05 and M06 for NH$_3$F$^+\cdots$CH$_3$ and NH$_2$F$_2^+\cdots$CH$_3$. Although M08-HX shows slightly larger monomer delocalization error than M05-2X and M06-2X, it is more reliable for predicting BEs of SEFB complexes, indicating that monomer delocalization error alone does not govern DFA performance. Bader's intermolecular delocalization indices (DIs) further support M08-HX as more reliable than M05-2X and M06-2X for SEFB, while the larger DIs from M05-2X and M06-2X explain the degraded performance of their dispersion-corrected variants for DF$\cdots$CH$_3$, with only a minor impact for DF$\cdots$NH$_3$, highlighting the system-dependent nature of functional delocalization error. Present work reveals that M08-HX is more reliable than M05-2X and M06-2X for open-shell DF$\cdots$CH$_3$ complexes, while all three functionals show comparable reliability for closed-shell DF$\cdots$NH$_3$ complexes.
