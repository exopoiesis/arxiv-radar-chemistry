Biases and Limits of AlphaFold Predictions for Non-Globular Proteins

Maria Cristina Aspromonte, Alexander M. Monzon, Miguel A. Andrade-Navarro, Oriol Bárcenas, Viktor Bartosik, Pau Bernadó, Juan Cortés, Zsuzsanna Dosztanyi, Gabor Erdos, Isabella C. Felli, Helena Gradišar, San Hadži, Tamás Hegedűs, Khalil Joron, Pavel Kaderavek, Eitan Lerner, Dirk Linke, Pablo Mier, Ana Pantelić, Roberta Pierattelli, Salvador Ventura, Marija Vidović, Metaxia Vlassi, Lukas Zidek, Silvio C. E. Tosatto

arXiv:2611.24546·q-bio.BM·Published 2026-11-18·Updated 2026-11-20

AlphaFold has transformed protein structure prediction, making structural models accessible across the life sciences. We explore experimentally validated cases of non-globular proteins to uncover where predictions diverge from biologically relevant structural states and how molecular context shapes these discrepancies, guiding a more informed interpretation of AlphaFold models.
