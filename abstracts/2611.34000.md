# Wannier-Based Low-Energy Electronic Structure Analysis of $λ$-(STF)$_2$GaCl$_4$

**Authors:** Arifa D. Agustin, Aulia A. Firdaus, Fathan M. Santana, Shuhei Fukuoka, Atsushi Kawamoto, Muhammad R. Ramadhan, Isao Watanabe, Dita P. Sari, Yasuyuki Ishii

The low-energy electronic structures of $λ$-(BETS)$_2$GaCl$_4$ and $λ$-(STF)$_2$GaCl$_4$ were investigated using density functional theory (DFT) and maximally localized Wannier functions (MLWFs). For $λ$-(STF)$_2$GaCl$_4$, DFT-selected and directly XRD-refined structural models were compared before and after fixed-lattice relaxation. Their initially different near-Fermi-level electronic structures become similar after relaxation. Relative to BETS, relaxed STF has a narrower frontier bandwidth while retaining a substantial dimerization energy scale. Two MLWFs were constructed for each selected unrelaxed structure to represent the upper frontier bands, with total spreads of 36.708 Å$^2$ for BETS and 37.578 Å$^2$ for STF. The Wannier-derived transfer integrals show a pronounced reduction of $t_{d1}$ from 51 meV in BETS to 6.5 meV in STF, indicating selective modification of the low-energy electronic connectivity.
