Defective $α$-In$_2$Se$_3$ from first principles

Jorge Vega Bazantes, Timo Lebeda, Zhiqiang Mao, Jianwei Sun

arXiv:2611.15060·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-11

$α$-In$_2$Se$_3$ is a two-dimensional van der Waals material with promising applications involving ferroelectricity. However, the presence of native defects can significantly influence its structural and electronic properties. In this work, we employ density functional theory with a state-of-the-art meta-generalized gradient approximation (meta-GGA) exchange-correlation functional to investigate the electronic structure of bulk $α$-In$_2$Se$_3$ and the formation of neutral point defects, including vacancies and antisites, under Se-rich and In-rich growth conditions. Our calculations reproduce the experimental structural parameters of the pristine material and reveal that the Se vacancy in the middle layer exhibits an unexpectedly low formation energy under both chemical environments. Structural analysis shows that the neighboring tetrahedrally coordinated In atom undergoes a substantial local relaxation along the three-fold axis, which preserves its $C_{3v}$ site symmetry even as its coordination is reduced from four to three, providing a microscopic explanation for the remarkable stability of this defect.
