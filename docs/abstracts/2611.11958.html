OriDynamics: Towards a World Model for Biomolecular Dynamics

Jiahao Yang, Dong Liu, Hongliang Li, Ji Chen, Yuehua Liu, Daoyun Ding, Duo An, Jun Li, Jiahua Rao, Ziqiao Zhang, Peicong Lin, Shuangjia Zheng

arXiv:2611.11958·q-bio.BM·Published 2026-11-09

Biomolecular recognition and regulation depend on atomic interactions that change as molecular partners fluctuate and adopt alternative conformations. Modeling these processes requires ensembles that capture both conformational diversity and coordinated atomic fluctuations between interacting molecules. Here we present OriDynamics, a unified framework for all-atom ensemble generation across protein monomers and complexes with small molecules or protein partners. OriDynamics introduces a latent ensemble decomposition that couples broad conformational exploration in a compact structural latent space with local all-atom sampling around generated proposals. Ensemble-level supervision constrains mean positions, fluctuation amplitudes and joint coordinate covariance to capture coordinated atomic variability within and between molecular partners. Across benchmarks of proteins and their complexes, OriDynamics demonstrates superior overall performance in conformational coverage, atomic fluctuation fidelity and interaction geometry compared with leading baselines. Application studies further demonstrate recovery of alternative functional conformations and partner-dependent changes in conformational ensembles alongside sampling of configurations associated with ligand dissociation. These capabilities provide a foundation for a biomolecular world model that represents accessible molecular states and their dependence on interaction context.
