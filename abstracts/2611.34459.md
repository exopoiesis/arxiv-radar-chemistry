# Uphill Diffusion in Yukawa Plasmas

**Authors:** Matt E. Caplan, Evan B. Bauer, Mohammed Bettioua

In a fluid mixture of many species an individual species can diffuse toward higher concentration without any external driving force because its flux is coupled to the gradients of other components. Such `uphill diffusion' is well known in the chemical and metallurgy literature but to our knowledge has not been studied for strongly coupled plasmas in neutron star and white dwarf interiors. We have previously developed a semi-analytic diffusion model that accurately predicts diffusion coefficients of individual species in a mixture in equilibrium. Uphill diffusion allows us to test this model out of equilibrium, and also test a generalized Darken relation for predicting diffusivities. We derive an analytic theory and compare to three-component molecular dynamics simulations and find very good agreement. This provides a novel out-of-equilibrium test of the generalization of our diffusion model to arbitrary multi-component mixtures.
