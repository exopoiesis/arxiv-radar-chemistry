A Universal Diffusion Model for the Quantum Position Operator

Ransell D'Souza, Austin Zadoks, Riccardo Dal Molin, Nataliia Manko, Junfeng Qiao, Nicola Marzari, Antimo Marrazzo

arXiv:2611.24130·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-18

The quantum position operator and its response to perturbations underpin the functional properties of insulating materials. In the modern theory of polarization, Wannier charge centres encode Berry-phase polarization, while their structural derivatives determine key response functions. Here we introduce a universal diffusion model based on E(3)-equivariant graph neural networks for moments of the position operator, with the first moment defining Wannier centres and the second central moment their real-space localization. We demonstrate the framework for liquid water, amorphous silicon and crystalline BaTiO$_3$, and train a universal model that generalizes to 100 unseen crystals spanning a 67-species chemical space. The predicted centres reproduce infrared spectra in large liquid and amorphous cells beyond routine first-principles reach and enable finite-difference calculations of Born effective charges and piezoelectric tensors. This framework provides a scalable route to electronic polarization and localization in complex materials, supporting high-throughput screening and finite-temperature simulations.
