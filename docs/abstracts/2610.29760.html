Nuclear Geometry-Driven Femtoscopic Correlations in Light-Ion Collisions

Dániel Kincses, Bithika Karmakar, Iurii Karpenko, Boris Tomášik

arXiv:2610.29760·nucl-th·Published 2026-10-21

Recent anisotropic flow measurements in $^{16}\rm O{+}^{16}\rm O$ and $^{20}\rm Ne{+}^{20}\rm Ne$ collisions at a center-of-mass energy per nucleon pair of 5.36~TeV provide evidence for nuclear geometry-driven collectivity. In this Letter, we investigate the femtoscopic signatures of nuclear geometry in these light-ion collision systems, using a hybrid simulation framework that couples \textit{ab initio} nuclear configurations to viscous hydrodynamics and hadronic transport. We show that the $\rm Ne{+}Ne/O{+}O$ ratio of the freeze-out eccentricity, extracted from azimuthally sensitive femtoscopy, reaches 1.5-1.8 in central collisions for deformed nuclear configurations, compared with about unity for a spherical baseline. This enhancement is significantly larger than that of elliptic flow in the same simulations, and remains stable when pre-equilibrium free streaming is omitted, even though the absolute source radii change considerably. Confronting these predictions with future data will provide new, complementary constraints on nuclear structure and initial-condition modeling.
