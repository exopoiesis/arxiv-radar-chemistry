Mapping the Phase Space of Cubic Chiral Semiconductors: High-Throughput Search,Stability, and Prototype Families

Pierre-Paul De Breuck, Hai-Chen Wang, Reynaldo Mares-Gil, Miguel A. L. Marques, Aldo H. Romero

arXiv:2611.18161·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-13

Chiral crystals show unique electronic, magnetic, and optical characteristics that make them attractive for topological and spintronic applications. Nevertheless, the limited set of identified chiral semiconductors, especially those with cubic symmetry, has so far constrained their technological application. In this work, we perform a high-throughput computational search for semiconducting chiral compounds in the cubic space group $P4_332$ (No. 212). By integrating machine-learning techniques with density functional theory, we obtain 24\,227 cubic chiral structures containing up to five elements, and identify 368 thermodynamically stable semiconductors on the convex hull and 3\,048 within 50~meV/atom. We analyze their structural motifs, stability, and electronic band structures, highlighting recurring prototype families, several of which show intrinsic intermediate-band features. Our results substantially expand the known phase space of cubic chiral semiconductors and may guide future efforts in the discovery and design of chiral materials.
