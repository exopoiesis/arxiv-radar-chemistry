Analytic $GW$-Bethe--Salpeter Gradients with Quasiparticle Response in the Resolution of the Identity

Christof Holzer

arXiv:2611.12617·physics.chem-ph·Published 2026-11-09

Analytic nuclear gradients for excited states from the Bethe$-$Salpeter equation (BSE) in the static screening approximation are presented within this work. The response of the screened interaction to nuclear displacements is treated within the resolution-of-the-identity (RI) approximation, in close analogy to RI random phase approximation gradients, and is folded into the Lagrangian formalism of time-dependent density functional theory gradients. Quasiparticle gradients enter via the full analytic derivative of the corrections for RI contour-deformation $G_0W_0$ and ev$GW$, also taking into account that incomplete quasiparticle spectra are typically supplied. Comparisons to earlier approaches using a geometry-independent fixed quasiparticle correction are made. For the latter, the orbital-rotation terms are derived exactly, too. The implemented gradient scales as $\mathcal{O}(N^4)$, and the three-index integrals are processed in batches with a memory demand of $\mathcal{O}(N_\mathrm{aux}^2 + N_\mathrm{occ}^2 N_\mathrm{aux})$. Excited-state geometries of a set of small molecules are compared to EOM-CCSD references and to TD-DFT. It is shown that the full quasiparticle gradient removes most of the systematic underestimation of the bond lengths in singlet excited states. A comparison along the C$-$C stretch of acetylene shows that the neglected derivative of the quasiparticle corrections is modest, but becomes erratic for $G_0W_0$ near poles of the self-energy, which ev$GW$ avoids.
