Determination of $V_{ud}$ from superallowed $β$ decays using effective field theory

Samuel Novario, Graham Chambers-Wall, Vincenzo Cirigliano, Wouter Dekens, Jordy de Vries, Stefano Gandolfi, Martin Hoferichter, Emanuele Mereghetti

arXiv:2611.18830·nucl-th·Published 2026-11-13

A precision determination of $V_{ud}$ from superallowed $β$ decays requires exquisite control over radiative corrections, including nuclear-structure-dependent effects whose evaluation has traditionally relied on nuclear models. To assess the uncertainties inherent in the nuclear matrix elements, an alternative strategy based on effective field theory (EFT) has been established in recent years. Here, we present material progress in this program, in terms of a first EFT calculation of the crucial correction $\barδ_\text{NS}$ for 12 superallowed transitions ranging from $^{10}$C $\rightarrow$ $^{10}$B to $^{54}$Co $\rightarrow$ $^{54}$Fe. These main results rely on nuclear ab initio calculations using the coupled cluster method, benchmarked against Quantum Monte Carlo evaluations for light nuclei. The global fit to superallowed decay rates reveals that the precision in $V_{ud}$ is currently limited by short-distance contributions that in the EFT are parameterized as two-nucleon contact interactions, leading to an uncertainty $δV_{ud}\simeq 6\times 10^{-4}$. We discuss the consequences for the status of the first-row unitarity test of the CKM matrix as well as a roadmap how to reduce the uncertainty in $V_{ud}$ in the future.
