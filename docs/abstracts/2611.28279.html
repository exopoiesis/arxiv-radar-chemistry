First-Principles Investigation of the {ScSe2} TMDs Monolayer: Structural Stability, Electronic and Optical Properties

Takwa Ben Othman, Mohsen Jemaï, Said Ridene

arXiv:2611.28279·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-20

We present an ab initio investigation of the {ScSe2} monolayer using density functional theory (DFT), as employed in the CP2K code, to assess its structural stability and electronic properties. The electronic behavior of both phases is governed by the local coordination environment of scandium and the associated lifting of degeneracy of its $3d$ orbitals. The valence bands (VBs) are dominated by {Se}$-4p$ states in both phases, while the states near the Fermi level distinguish the metallic 1T phase from the semiconducting 2H phase. Optical response calculations reveal markedly different spectral signatures for the two polymorphs. Two findings emerge from this study: a polymorphism-driven metal-to-semiconductor transition without any change in chemical composition and distinct optical responses relevant to phase-selective optoelectronic devices.
