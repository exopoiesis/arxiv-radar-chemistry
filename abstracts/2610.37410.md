# Accurate structural modelling of protein-glycan recognition with GlycoWeave

**Authors:** Huifeng Zhao, Jintu Zhang, Yifei Xiao, Youqiao Qian, Silong Zhai, Zichang Jin, Li Wang, Yuchao Wang, Hang Dong, Yifan Zhao, Zhenxing Wu, Hongyan Du, Yuansheng Huang, Bowei Zhao, Xiaorui Wang, Shukai Gu, Jike Wang, Zhongjun Li, Zhongtang Li, Tingjun Hou, Yu Kang

Protein-glycan recognition underlies diverse biological processes, yet accurate prediction of protein-glycan complex structures remains challenging owing to the chemical diversity, conformational flexibility and sparse experimental structural coverage of glycans. Here we present GlycoWeave, a glycan-specific diffusion framework that integrates chemically constrained conformational sampling, multiscale molecular representations and multi-source learning. By operating in internal-coordinate space, GlycoWeave preserves glycan connectivity and stereochemistry while efficiently exploring bound conformations. A heterogeneous equivariant network couples atom-level interaction geometry with monosaccharide-level structural context, whereas multi-source, multi-head training integrates experimental complexes with structures refined using classical and quantum mechanics-informed potentials. On a benchmark of 698 protein-glycan complexes, GlycoWeave achieves a 71.9% Top-25 high-quality success rate, compared with 19.2% for AlphaFold 3, while requiring only 0.34 s per sampled pose. GlycoWeave further preserves glycan structural validity, recovers native protein-glycan interactions and reproduces linkage-specific conformational preferences, maintaining high accuracy across diverse glycan architectures and protein environments. These results establish GlycoWeave as an accurate, efficient and chemically faithful framework for modelling protein-glycan recognition. GlycoWeave is freely available at [https://cadd.zju.edu.cn/glycoweave](https://cadd.zju.edu.cn/glycoweave).
