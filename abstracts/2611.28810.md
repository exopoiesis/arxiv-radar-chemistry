# Learning free energy across a differentiable temperature range by deep generative modeling

**Authors:** Yaowen Zhang, Chen Chen, Wanyu Lin, Shuo-Hui Li, Ding Pan

Free energy governs the directionality, stability, and temperature sensitivity of physical, chemical, and biological transformations. Conventional free energy calculations, such as thermodynamic integration and free energy perturbation, often have to be performed at individual temperatures and are limited by the high cost of sampling an extensive set of thermodynamic states. Here, we introduce learned temperature-differentiable free energy perturbation (TD-FEP), a deep generative framework that learns a temperature-conditioned, invertible mapping between reference and target systems through data-free, energy-based optimization. TD-FEP thereby unifies one-shot state generation and free energy calculation across a continuous temperature range. We validated TD-FEP across a broad range of applications, including isolated molecules, periodic materials, and chemical reactions.
