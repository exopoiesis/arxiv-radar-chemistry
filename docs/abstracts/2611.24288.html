Reconciling Models for Infinite-Layer Nickelate Superconductors: Role of Interstitial-$s$ Band or $A$-Pocket

Ao Zhang, Chao Deng, Leonard M. Verhoff, Mi Jiang, Run Yang, Zhixiang Shi, Karsten Held, Liang Si

arXiv:2611.24288·cond-mat.supr-con·Published 2026-11-18

Understanding the minimal model that captures the essential physics of infinite-layer nickelate superconductors remains a central challenge. Using first-principles density-functional theory combined with dynamical mean-field theory (DFT+DMFT), we compute and compare the strongly correlated electronic structures and optical spectra of various effective models for the infinite-layer nickelate LaNiO$_2$, ranging from a $d_{x^2-y^2}$ 1-band model to a full 6-band model with all Ni-$3d$ plus an interstitial-$s$ orbital. The results demonstrate that Ni-$3d$ 5-band models overestimate the Hundness picture, whereas 6-band models, which explicitly include the interstitial-$s$ band and have an $A$-pocket, recover the characteristic Mottness physics. Importantly, the influence of the interstitial-$s$ band on the correlations of the $d_{x^2-y^2}$ band is negligible. Thus, superconductivity in infinite-layer nickelates can be reliably described within a single-band Hubbard framework, while the accurate description of inter-orbital properties such as (optical) conductivities requires the inclusion of at least the interstitial-$s$ band.
