# Compensated Grid-Based Hybrid Exchange - A Simple, Accurate, Efficient, and Differentiable Recipe for Large-Scale Molecular Systems

**Authors:** Edward G. Hohenstein, Melisa Alkan, Xiaojie Wu, Robert M. Parrish

A compensated grid-based approach is developed for hybrid exchange in large-scale molecular systems. The method uses a symmetrically overlap-fitted grid-based exchange approach, with fitting performed in the orthonormalized atomic orbital basis. This fitting is augmented with a superposition-of-atomic-densities difference-density (dSCF) partitioning of the exchange energy, yielding the finished compensated grid-based exchange approach. The complete method is found to have orders of magnitude reduction of absolute and relative error properties versus raw grid-based exchange, and remains analytically differentiable. The method is easy to implement and provides efficiency gains over GPU integral-direct techniques for high quality and/or augmented basis sets. Library functions to help users implement the method in their own quantum chemistry codes are provided in the recently-shipped cuEST v0.3 library.
