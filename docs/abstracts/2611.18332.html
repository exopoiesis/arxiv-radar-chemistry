Radiation in relativistic plasmas via particle simulations

Efstratios M. Kritikos, Pontus Svensson, Daniel Plummer, Brian Reville, Bob Bingham, Gianluca Gregori

arXiv:2611.18332·physics.plasm-ph·Published 2026-11-13

We investigate radiation in relativistic electron-proton isotropic plasmas using molecular dynamics, with velocity-dependent inertia, retarded interactions, and quantum-corrected pair potentials. Radiation spectra from the Liénard-Wiechert fields agree with analytical models. Successive scattering events suppress bremsstrahlung even at weak coupling, while jitter radiation becomes comparable at higher coupling for all resolved frequencies. This first-principles framework will help develop radiation models for suppression and field effects that are inaccessible in typical particle-in-cell methods.
