Machine Learning-Driven High-Throughput Screening of Equimolar and Non-Equimolar High-Entropy Carbides

Arina A. Svinukhova, Faisal Baber, Michel M. Lukanov, Viktor S. Baidyshev, Daria Terentieva, Alexander Ya. Pak, Alexander G. Kvashnin

arXiv:2611.25127·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-18

Search for optimal compositions among high-entropy ceramics are challenging due to large composition space, which are unable to test either experimentally or by \textit{ab initio} calculations. Here we present a high-throughput screening for equimolar and non-equimolar high-entropy carbides (HEC) among group IVB and VB transition metals (Ti, Zr, Hf, V, Nb and Ta). Large Atomistic Model MACE, and deep learning based interatomic potential DeePMD trained on \textit{ab initio}-based dataset of structures are used to effectively screen over the thousands of potential non-equimolar compositions among high-entropy carbides of Ti, Zr, Hf, V, Nb and Ta transition metals. A set of both equimolar and non-equimolar HECs are predicted to be stable in order to further synthesize those compositions based on the predictions.
