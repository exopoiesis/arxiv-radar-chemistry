# Time-gated optical-parametric-amplification and field-resolved detection of mid-infrared free-induction decay signals

**Authors:** Sergio Revuelta, Gerard Faustino, Ka Fai Mak, Ferenc Krausz, Nicholas Karpowicz

Infrared spectroscopy is a key tool to study complex structural dynamics in molecular systems. However, resolving weak or broad vibrational modes is challenging due to their rapid decay. Here, we demonstrate a technique to significantly extend the detectable lifetime - and thereby spectral resolution - of molecular signatures by employing synchronized time-gated optical parametric amplification of mid-infrared free-induction decay signals. Utilizing a Cr:ZnS laser system and a nonlinear ZnGeP2 crystal, we achieve a 10-fold increase in the electric field strength of the infrared molecular signal, yielding a two-orders of magnitude enhancement in dynamic range and a 2-fold increase in the signal-to-noise ratio. Using isopropyl alcohol as a model system, we demonstrate that OPA-enhanced detection extends the observable window of vibrational modes up to 72 %. This approach allows for the reconstruction of high-fidelity molecular fingerprints that resolve complex vibrational relaxation pathways and weak structural dynamics, previously hidden within the background and noise, providing a pathway for new high-sensitivity and time-resolved studies of complex chemical structures.
