Studying temperature-induced decrease of spontaneous polarization in PbTiO$_3$ from vibrational amplitude

Zhikai Gao, Yuanji Xu, Fuyang Tian

arXiv:2611.16266·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-12

PbTiO$_3$ is a typical ferroelectric polarization induced negative thermal expansion (NTE) material at a wide range of temperature. The elegant TiO$_6$ octahedron is used to study the polarization and NTE, whereas lacking its quantitative characterization. In this work, we propose a concise projection of vibrational amplitude to describe anisotropic distortion of atoms with temperature based on $ab$ $initio$ molecular dynamics simulations. We find that the huge negative mode Grüneisen coefficient is mainly from the different vibrational modes of the TiO$_6$ octahedral rotation at two boundary symmetry points in tetragonal phase along $c$ direction. The potential energy surface as a function of amplitude suggests the $c$-axis compression facilitates the TiO$_6$ octahedral rotation. We show that the vibrational mode of TiO$_6$ octahedral rotation around negative-expansion axis can promote spontaneous polarization reduction with increasing temperature, making transformation of PbTiO$_3$ from tetragonal to cubic phase occur, via the projected amplitude, potential energy surface, and forces of individual atoms.
