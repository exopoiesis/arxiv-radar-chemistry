# N-Polar AlGaN/GaN Heterojunction Bipolar Transistor

**Authors:** Md Saklain Morshed, John Cobbinah, Ijaas Mohamed, Jonathan Pratt, Akilesh Srikanth, Chandan Joishi, Siddharth Rajan

Heterojunction bipolar transistors based on the III-Nitride systems have significantly lower performance than field effect transistors due to challenges associated with p-type doping of the base. N-polar AlGaN/GaN HBTs can overcome these challenges by polarization-induced holes in the base and base-emitter interface. We experimentally demonstrate the first N-polar AlGaN/GaN heterojunction bipolar transistors (HBTs) grown by plasma-assisted molecular beam epitaxy on GaN substrate without emitter regrowth. The HBTs exhibit a peak current gain of approximately 15, peak differential current gain of approximately 18, and emitter-area-normalized peak current densities exceeding 20 kA/cm^2 and 66 kA/cm^2 for emitter areas of 10 x 10 um^2 and 3 x 10 um^2, respectively. The specific contact resistance for base is estimated 1.32 x 10^{-2} Ohm.cm^2. The open-base breakdown voltage is characterized ~63 V for a 300 nm collector without dielectric passivation. This work lays the foundation for future development of N-polar III-Nitride HBTs for next-generation RF applications.
