# Humidity-modulated Ostwald Step Rule in $\rm FAPbI_3$ Martensitic Transitions

**Authors:** Ping Tuo, Ju Li

Experimental observations reveal a dual role of moisture in $\rm FAPbI_3$ phase transitions. During fabrication at high temperatures, humidity improves the quality of the perovskite ($α$ phase) films, whereas at low service temperatures, humidity accelerates degradation of the photoactive $α$ phase into the photo-inactive hexagonal $δ$ phase. Despite extensive experimental evidence, the microscopic mechanisms by which moisture modulates phase stability and transition pathways remain unknown. Here, using large-scale atomistic simulations powered by an ab initio machine-learned potential, we reproduce the experimentally observed increase in the $δ$-to-$α$ transition temperature under increased humidity. Our simulations reveal a layered metastable intermediate along the Burgers transition pathway that exhibits a strong affinity for water. The competing water uptake of this intermediate and the $δ$ phase governs the humidity dependence of transition kinetics. These findings elucidate the dual role of moisture in perovskite phase transitions and demonstrate how environmental species can stabilize metastable intermediates and reshape transition pathways, providing an environment-dependent perspective on Ostwald's step rule.
