Evidential Deep Regression for Universal Density Functional Theory Hamiltonian Models

Jiaxuan Liu, Yan Zhang, Caiyuan Ye, Tiannian Zhu, Zhong Fang, Hongming Weng, Quansheng Wu

arXiv:2611.15786·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-11

Universal density functional theory (DFT) Hamiltonian models trained across broad chemical and structural spaces offer an efficient route from atomic structure to electronic properties. However, estimating prediction uncertainty alongside fast Hamiltonian predictions remains a challenge. In this work, we present EviDTB, an evidential deep regression approach for tight-binding DFT Hamiltonians that effectively estimates prediction uncertainty in a single forward pass. Compared with deep ensembles, Monte Carlo dropout, and mean-variance estimation (MVE), EviDTB achieves a better balance between computational cost and uncertainty-quantification performance. On 5,478 held-out test structures, EviDTB achieves a full spin--orbit-coupled (SOC) Hamiltonian matrix-element mean absolute error of 0.622meV. Its mean uncertainty per atom correlates with the mean local squared prediction error (Spearman $ρ=0.812$); removing the 15% most uncertain structures reduces the 99th percentile of this error by 78%, from 16.63 to 3.72eV$^2$. This score offers a practical way to prioritize candidates for first-principles verification. We hope that combining fast Hamiltonian prediction with uncertainty-guided screening will accelerate the search for materials with desirable electronic properties.
