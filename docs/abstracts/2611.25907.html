Ab-initio study of magnetic interaction in RNiO2: role of rare-earth size and epitaxial strain

Ramasamy Murugesan, Shiyu Deng, Marie-Bernadette Lepetit

arXiv:2611.25907·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-19

In this paper we present a systematic first-principles investigation of the structure and nearest-neighbor magnetic exchange interactions in the infinite-layer RNiO2 thin-film series (R = La-Lu), under the constrain of different substrates. Density functional theory calculations are first employed to optimize the crystal structures, while CAS+DDCI correlated wave-function calculations were used for the determination of the magnetic exchanges. This methodology allows to obtain magnetic interactions in good agreement with RIXS experimental estimations. In the pristine case, our results confirm that the structure of RNiO2 (except for R = La and Ce) adopts the I4/mcm space group, characterized by an out-of-plane rotation of the NiO4 plaquettes. This result remains valid under substrate constrains, even if compressive strain reduces the amplitude of the rotational distortion and leads to an enhancement of the magnetic exchange interaction relative to the pristine structures. The latter are found to depend sensitively on both the rare-earth ionic radius and the applied epitaxial strain. These results highlight the role of structural distortions and substrate-induced strain in tuning magnetic interactions in infinite-layer nickelates.
