# ENGRAM: Reliability-Weighted Latent Fusion and Occlusion-Aware Layered Readout for Video World Models

**Authors:** Chenhao Wu, Zhaolong Su, Yang Liu, Tianrui Su, Yingguang Yang, Shang Luo, Yancheng Chen, Chongyang Zhang, Hao Zheng, Kefu Xu, Congjing Ran, Bin Chong

Camera-conditioned video world models aim to generate 3D-consistent videos from a single image along specified camera trajectories. However, set-union updates in latent spatial memory leave repeated observations unconsolidated, while frontmost-only readout limits simultaneous access to stored evidence at different depths. To address these limitations, we present ENGRAM, a latent spatial memory framework combining reliability-weighted latent fusion and occlusion-aware layered readout. Specifically, recursive fusion integrates geometrically and visually compatible observations in a channel-normalized latent space, consolidating repeated evidence into a shared scene representation. Layered readout exposes multiple depth-ordered latent candidates with their reliability and availability at each target cell. This gives the generator access to information from multiple surfaces as visibility changes. Explicit camera rays provide viewpoint information independently of memory coverage, while geometric consistency constraints guide memory updates. Memory retrieval uses a single projection at latent resolution, avoiding intermediate RGB rendering and VAE re-encoding. We adapt a pretrained video generator using only 256 training scenes. On 100 RealEstate10K test scenes, ENGRAM improves novel-view-synthesis and closed-loop PSNRs over Spatia by 0.82 and 4.48 dB, respectively. On a 200-case WorldScore evaluation subset, it achieves an average score of 67.78, exceeding Spatia by 3.31 points.
