Density and excess molar enthalpy of 2-butanol + glyme liquid mixtures. Application of the DISQUAC, UNIFAC (Dortmund) and ERAS models to 2-alkanol + linear ether systems

Fatemeh Pazoki, João Victor Alves-Laurentino, Susana Villa, Daniel Lozano-Martín, Fernando Hevia, Luis Felipe Sanz, Juan Antonio González

arXiv:2610.19805·physics.chem-ph·Published 2026-10-14

Densities and excess molar volumes ($V_{\text{m}}^{\text{E}}$) at (293.15 to 303.15) K and excess molar enthalpies ($H_{\text{m}}^{\text{E}}$) at 298.15 K are reported for the 2-butanol + 2,5-dioxahexane, or + 2,5,8-trioxanonane, or + 2,5,8,11-tetraoxadodecane, or + 2,5,8,11,14-pentaoxapentadecane systems at 95 kPa. Densities were measured using a densitometer Anton Paar DSA 5000, and excess enthalpies by means of a micro-calorimeter Tian-Calvet. $H_{\text{m}}^{\text{E}}$ and $V_{\text{m}}^{\text{E}}$ data are positive, indicating that interactions between like molecules are dominant and that the main contribution to $V_{\text{m}}^{\text{E}}$ is the interactional one. Our enthalpic data together with data from the literature have been used to evaluate the enthalpy of alcohol-glyme interactions, which are ranged between ($-$31 and $-$22) kJ mol$^{-1}$. From the comparison with $H_{\text{m}}^{\text{E}}$ results for glyme + heptane systems, and from the position of the investigated mixtures in the diagram $G_{\text{m}}^{\text{E}}$ (excess molar Gibbs energy) vs. $H_{\text{m}}^{\text{E}}$, it is shown that interactions are of dispersive type. 2-Alkanol + dipropyl ether (DPE), or + glyme systems have also been studied using the DISQUAC, UNIFAC (Dortmund) and ERAS models. According to ERAS results, physical interactions in glyme systems are dominant, while association effects are predominant in mixtures with DPE. The DISQUAC interaction parameters suggest that interactions related to the OH/O contacts are essentially dispersive. UNIFAC fails when representing thermodynamic properties of the studied solutions.
