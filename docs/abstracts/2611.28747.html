Minimal Three-Band Model and $d$-Wave Superconductivity in La$_4$Ni$_3$O$_8$

Ao Zhang, Chao Deng, Wenfeng Wu, Run Yang, Zhixiang Shi, Liang Si

arXiv:2611.28747·cond-mat.supr-con·Published 2026-11-21

Motivated by the recent observation of ambient-pressure superconductivity in Pr- and Y-substituted La$_4$Ni$_3$O$_8$, we combined density-functional theory (DFT), dynamical mean-field theory (DMFT), random phase approximation (RPA), and fluctuation exchange approximation (FLEX) methods to study its electronic structure and superconducting pairing symmetry. DFT band analysis indicates that the low-energy states are mainly dominated by three Ni-3$d_{x^2-y^2}$ orbitals with weak $d$-$p$ hybridization. Multi-orbital DMFT calculations yield an average $d_{x^2-y^2}$ filling of about 0.87, close to the optimal value for infinite-layer nickelates. By comparing the DMFT results of $d_{x^2-y^2}$ only and full Ni-3$d$ models, we find that their electronic properties are almost completely consistent, demonstrating the effectiveness of the simplified $d_{x^2-y^2}$ model. RPA and FLEX calculations based on this model consistently indicate a dominant $d_{x^2-y^2}$-wave pairing instability. These results establish La$_4$Ni$_3$O$_8$ as a system whose electronic structure closely resembles that of hole-doped cuprates, suggesting that the electronic pockets near the $Γ$- and $A$-points, which are characteristic of infinite-layer nickelates, may not be essential for superconductivity. The calculated superconducting transition temperature ($T_C$) is approximately 10\,K, in good agreement with experimental observations.
