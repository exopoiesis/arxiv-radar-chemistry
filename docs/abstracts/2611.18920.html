From RuP$_3$SiO$_{11}$ to Ideal Geometry: Parallel-Face Octahedra for Kitaev-Dominated Interactions

Do Hoon Kiem, Satoshi Okamoto, Tom Berlijn

arXiv:2611.18920·cond-mat.str-el·Published 2026-11-13

RuP$_3$SiO$_{11}$ has recently been identified as a Kitaev spin liquid candidate via neutron scattering. To assess its proximity to the Kitaev limit, we perform \textit{ab initio} calculations of magnetic interactions, finding good agreement for the Kitaev and off-diagonal exchanges, while the Heisenberg exchange is ferromagnetic rather than the experimentally fitted antiferromagnetic one. We also identify a sizable antiferromagnetic out-of-plane exchange not captured in previous analyses. Extending the study to eight substitutional analogs in the $MT_3Q$O$_{11}$ family ($M$ = Ru, Os; $T$ = P, As; $Q$= Si, Ge), we observe strong variations in magnetic interactions, highlighting the tunability of this materials class. Finally, inspired by RuP$_3$SiO$_{11}$, which exhibits a near parallel-face octahedral arrangement, we compare idealized geometries and show that the parallel-face configuration yields a Kitaev-dominated regime, suggesting a new structural route to the Kitaev limit.
