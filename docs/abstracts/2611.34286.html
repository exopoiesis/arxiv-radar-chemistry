Collective relaxation dynamics and vitrification in hexatic smectic binary equimolar mixtures containing component with varying C$_3$F$_7$CH$_2$OC$_m$H$_{2m}$- chain length

Aleksandra Deptuch, Mateusz Pączek, Anna Drzewicz, Marcin Piwowarczyk, Magdalena Urbańska, Ewa Juszyńska-Gałązka

arXiv:2611.34286·cond-mat.soft·Published 2026-11-25

Differential scanning calorimetry, polarizing optical microscopy, and broadband dielectric spectroscopy are applied for investigation of phase transitions in equimolar binary mixtures consisting of the MHPOBC liquid crystal as one component and 3FmHPhH6 homologs as a second component with different C$_3$F$_7$CH$_2$OC$_m$H$_{2m}$- chain length (m = 2-7). The paraelectric smectic A* - ferroelectric smectic C* - antiferroelectric smectic C$_A$* - hexatic smectic X$_A$* - glassy smectic X$_A$* sequence is observed for all mixtures. The appearance of the bond-orientational order in smectic X$_A$* is accompanied by an additional relaxation process P$_{hex}$ in dielectric spectra at the low-frequency side of the P$_H$ phason, consistent with its assignment to bond-orientational fluctuations. The empirical exponential-power relationship is proposed for the ratio between the relaxation times of P$_{hex}$ and P$_H$. The behavior of the structural $α$-relaxation and secondary $β$-relaxation in smectic X$_A$* depends on the m parameter, changing from overlapping $α$ and $β$ processes for m = 2 to well-separated for m = 6, 7. A double glass transition is observed by calorimetry on heating for m = 5-7. The calorimetric glass transition temperature shows a composition-dependent correspondence with temperatures at which either the P$_H$ or $α$-relaxation time reaches 100 s, suggesting that different relaxation processes may contribute to vitrification.
