Density functional theory of renormalization group in nuclear matter

Yong-rui Chen, Yang-yang Tan, Wei-jie Fu

arXiv:2611.24345·nucl-th·Published 2026-11-18

We present the density functional renormalization group (density-fRG), in which density fluctuations are separated from the quantum fluctuations in the vacuum and are integrated out successively within the functional renormalization group approach. This allows us to quantify medium effects and to study the physics of high baryon densities. The method is applied to the nucleon-meson effective field theory, also known as the Walecka model, where the density dependence of the nucleon-meson Yukawa couplings is generated self-consistently by their flow equations. It is found that both the attractive and the repulsive nucleon-meson interactions are screened by the dense medium. This results in an equation of state of symmetric nuclear matter that is stiffer than in the relativistic mean-field approximation for $ρ_0\lesssimρ\lesssim 2.5\,ρ_0$, and becomes softer for $ρ\gtrsim 2.5\,ρ_0$, where $ρ_0$ is the saturation density. Furthermore, a new phenomenon called the locking of the Fermi surface is found: the effective energy of the quasi-nucleon stays close to the Fermi surface, while both are running with the renormalization group scale.
