# Ab Initio Molecular Dynamics-Guided Machine-Learning Potentials for Mapping Nitric Oxide Interactions with Oxygen-Covered Graphite

**Authors:** Yaideny Rodriguez Linares, Gilberto A. Alou Angulo, Samuel Del Fré, Maurice Monnerville, Alejandro Rivero Santamaría

Exploratory ab initio molecular dynamics is used to construct machine-learning interatomic potentials for NO interactions with highly oriented pyrolytic graphite at 33\% bridge-site oxygen coverage. Representative configurations sampled across surface temperatures and incidence energies provide reference energies and forces for training and validation. The selected potential enables relaxation of 300 adsorption configurations and construction of spatial and angular interaction maps. These maps reveal increasingly restricted attractive regions during vertical approach and reduced short-range repulsion upon molecular reorientation. The resulting adsorption geometries and interaction landscapes guide further first-principles sampling toward refining potentials for NO oxidation dynamics.
