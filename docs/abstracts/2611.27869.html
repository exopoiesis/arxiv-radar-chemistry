Linear Response Theory of Nitrogen K-Edge X-ray Absorption of TCNQ

Alberto Boccuni, Vita Ilakovac, Stéphane Carniato, Jacques Kontak-Desmarais

arXiv:2611.27869·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-20

The linear response of the density is computed in the frequency domain within the generalized Kohn--Sham framework of adiabatic time-dependent density functional theory, employing hybrid exchange-correlation functionals. It is shown that the generalized Casida equations imply a Dyson-like equation for the linear-response of the full frequency-dependent one-electron reduced density matrix of the reference GKS system. We implement this formalism in the \textsc{Crystal} code and apply it to the nitrogen K-edge near-edge X-ray absorption fine structure (NEXAFS) of 7,7,8,8-tetracyano-$p$-quinodimethane (TCNQ), the acceptor in the charge-transfer salt TTF-TCNQ. We interpret the spectrum through a step-by-step construction by building blocks of increasing complexity. This procedure separates the effects of $π$ conjugation, of multiple equivalent absorbing centers and of the ring on the in-plane ($π_\parallel$) and out-of-plane ($π_\perp$) resonances. The theoretical result is confirmed by a comparison with angle-resolved experiments. In contrast with earlier assignments based only on the ground-state density of states, we find that the lowest-energy resonance is dominated by the ground-state KS LUMO contribution. Indeed, comparison with the independent-particle approximation shows that the exact-exchange contribution to the kernel is essential for a correct assignment of the peaks.
