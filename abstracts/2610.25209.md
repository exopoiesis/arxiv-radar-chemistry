# One Component Plasmas Frequency Moments from the ISF

**Authors:** D. Ballester

Dynamic structural characteristics of classical one component plasmas (OCP) are an essential tool for understanding of strongly coupled plasmas through experiments and numerical simulations. Here, instead of the dynamic structure factor, we focus our attention on the intermediate scattering function (ISF). Theoretical results are compared to those of obtained by the method of molecular dynamics (MD).
