Structural, optoelectronic, mechanical, thermodynamic and thermoelectric properties of kesterite Cu2ZnSnS4: A first-principles study

Iskandar Raufzoda, Amondulloi Burhonzoda, Dilshod Nematov, Makhmadzoir Kayumov, Saidjafar Murodzoda, Anushervon Ashurov, Sakhidod Sattorzoda, Makhsud Barot Islomzoda, Kholmirzo Kholmurodov, Shuwei Tang

arXiv:2611.28172·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-20

The structural, optoelectronic, mechanical, thermodynamic, and thermoelectric properties of kesterite Cu2ZnSnS4 were investigated using density functional theory. The mBJ and HSE06 approaches yield band gaps of 0.88 and 1.55 eV, respectively. Optical calculations reveal high absorption, with the onset in the 0.8-1.0 eV range. Hydrostatic compression from 0 to 30 kbar decreases the lattice parameters and increases the band gap. The elastic constants satisfy the Born stability criterion, and B/G $>$ 1.75 indicates plastic behavior. Phonon calculations confirm dynamic stability. In the temperature range from 0 to 1000 K, the Helmholtz free energy decreases, the vibrational entropy increases, and the heat capacity approaches a plateau above 500 K. The thermoelectric properties were investigated in the temperature range from 100 to 1000 K. The Seebeck coefficient remains positive, peaking at approximately 310 $μ$V/K at 250 K and decreasing to 239 $μ$V/K at 1000 K. These results encourage the investigation of Cu2ZnSnS4 as a good candidate for thermoelectric power conversion.
