Quantum Amplitude Estimation for Ligand and Pose Index Retrieval

Pei-Kun Yang

arXiv:2611.30028·quant-ph·Published 2026-11-23

Structure-based virtual screening spans a combinatorial configuration space, motivating a quantum screening framework that targets threshold-qualified configuration indices without reconstructing a complete interaction-score table. Quantum amplitude estimation based on quantum phase estimation (QAE/QPE) supplies finite-resolution phase information to a reversible comparator that writes its decision to FLAG, while optional Grover-style quantum selection (QSEL) amplifies FLAG-selected outcomes and selector measurements identify the ligand, receptor state, rotation, and translation. In an ideal logical benchmark using AutoDock grid interactions as a proof-of-concept model, we evaluated initial FLAG acceptance probability, classification precision, shots required for 95% FLAG detection, and logical Q-operator calls across eight receptor systems and nested libraries of up to 1,024 ligand occupancies. Higher QAE/QPE resolution generally improved classification precision at additional evaluation cost, whereas suitably chosen QSEL iteration counts reduced required shots without improving classification precision, revealing selection and resource trade-offs for extending coherent index retrieval to more physically detailed molecular evaluation models.
