Double-Bracket Quantum Algorithms for Preparing Multiple Excited States

Salahuddin Abdul Rahman, Özkan Karabacak, Rafal Wisniewski

arXiv:2611.22774·quant-ph·Published 2026-11-17

Many problems in quantum chemistry, many-body physics, and optimization require excited states of a Hamiltonian. Double-bracket quantum algorithms compile circuits recursively from evolutions under the Hamiltonian and a second operator, without a parameterized ansatz. They have been proposed for several tasks, such as diagonalization and the preparation of ground and thermal states. In this work, we extend this framework to excited states. Combining the ensemble approach with double-bracket flows, we introduce the Double-Bracket quantum algorithm for Multiple EXcited states (DB-MEX), which evolves $p+1$ orthonormal states toward low-lying eigenstates of the Hamiltonian with a single recursively compiled quantum circuit, so they remain orthonormal by construction. The circuit approximates a double-bracket flow of a weighted sum of projectors onto the evolving states, whose number and weights are design parameters. The number of states sets the highest targeted excited state, and the weights determine whether the algorithm prepares the $p+1$ lowest eigenstates, only the $p$-th excited state, or only the span of the $p+1$ lowest eigenstates. In the latter case, a post-processing step recovers the individual eigenstates from their span. We show that the double-bracket flow and, for small step sizes, its discretization converge to the target for almost all initial states, with explicit local rates given by products of weight and energy gaps.
