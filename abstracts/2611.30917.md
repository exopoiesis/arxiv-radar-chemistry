# Molecular Pawns on a Surface Chessboard: Computational Investigation of Nanographenes with Atomistic Methods

**Authors:** Antonio Lorenzo Mariano, Jacopo Lupi, Emilio Lorini, Matteo Briganti

Atomically precise nanographenes combine tunable electronic topology with localized, coherent spin states, making them candidate platforms for molecular quantum technologies. This review examines the multiscale theoretical framework governing these systems: the hierarchy of computational methods for describing magnetic properties, the substrate-molecule interface, on-surface synthetic kinetics, and supramolecular self-assembly. We highlight how density functional theory, automated reaction-path discovery, and spin-aware machine-learned interatomic potentials connect static electronic structure to mesoscale dynamics, and how magnetic exchange, optical excitations, and spin transport can be predicted before synthesis.
