Collisional lineshapes of an N$_2$-perturbed electronic transition in O$_2$ from first principles of quantum theory validated by accurate experimental spectra

Maciej Gancewski, Erin Adkins, Hubert Jóźwiak, Nikodem Stolarczyk, Ernesto Quintas-Sánchez, Richard Dawes, Joseph T. Hodges, Piotr Wcisło

arXiv:2611.22649·physics.atom-ph·Published 2026-11-17

We demonstrate the feasibility of predicting the lineshape of an electronic molecular transition collisionally perturbed by another molecule from first principles of quantum mechanics. We consider an N$_2$-perturbed O$_2$ $A$-band transition, which is relevant to spectroscopic observations in Earth's atmosphere. This approach simultaneously incorporates the electronic structure, quantum scattering and lineshape effects required to accurately predict pressure- and temperature-dependence of observed lineshapes. The $\textit{ab initio}$ lineshapes reproduce measurements to better than 0.6%. Theory also provides new physical constraints on beyond-Voigt lineshape parameters in fits of high-precision data, yielding fit residuals at the 0.08% level. This work motivates advances in molecular electronic structure calculations and demonstrates the potential to generate comprehensive spectroscopic reference data from first principles.
