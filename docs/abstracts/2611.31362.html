Band-Selective Coupling to Charge Density Wave Fluctuations in Kagome Superconductors AV3Sb5

Peter C. Moen, T. G. H. Blank, C. Le, S. Smit, N. Heinsdorf, S. Sarkar, C. C. Au-Yeung, V. Zimmermann, J. Kraan, C. S. B. Pang, M. Tuniz, M. Bluschke, A. A. Haghighirad, I. Rajput, A. C. Salinas, S. Gorovikov, P. Dosanjh, J. I. Dadap, A. K. Mills, S. Zhdanovich, M. Michiardi, G. Levy, I. S. Elfimov, S. D. Wilson, M. Le Tacon, D. J. Jones, X. Wu, A. Damascelli

arXiv:2611.31362·cond-mat.supr-con·Published 2026-11-23

The kagome superconductors AV3Sb5 (A=K, Rb, Cs) host multiband superconductivity (SC) within an unconventional charge density wave (CDW) parent phase, yet the relationship between the two remains unresolved. Through time- and angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (tr-ARPES) experiments on CsV3Sb5 and KV3Sb5, we observe coherent band oscillations driven by coupling to a low-energy CDW amplitude mode at 3.1 THz. We find this coupling to be highly band-selective, differing by a factor of over 2 even between bands with dominant V-3d character. We reproduce these results with frozen-phonon density functional theory calculations, offering insight towards the relation between differing Fermi surface characters and our observed band selectivity. Furthermore, we observe differences in coupling channels between KV3Sb5 and CsV3Sb5. These results establish CDW amplitude mode fluctuations as a band-selective low-energy coupling channel that intimately contributes to the interplay between CDW and SC in the AV3Sb5 compounds.
