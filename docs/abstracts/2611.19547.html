Affordable Analytic Gradients for G0W0-BSE Excited States for Multiple GW Variants via Back-Propagation

Qirui Gao, Igor Ying Zhang, Xin Xu

arXiv:2611.19547·physics.chem-ph·Published 2026-11-14

We derive, implement and validate analytic nuclear gradients for three variants of $GW$ self-energies---the contour-deformation (CD), the analytic continuation (AC), and the contour deformation with $W$-analytic-continuation (CD-WAC)---and for the Bethe--Salpeter equation (BSE) built on top of them. All gradients are expressed as contractions of a set of covectors connected to derivatives of primitive quantities in SCF calculations. The derivation of these covectors is a back-propagation procedure through the $GW$ and BSE equations. Central finite differences confirm the correctness of all implemented gradients. With the AC-$GW$-BSE gradient we optimize the excited-state geometries of carbon monoxide, a donor-acceptor dye labelled as DA1, and DMABN, a typical model system for twisted intramolecular charge-transfer phenomena (TICT), and have obtained results that successfully reproduce observations from experiments or high-level calculations. We also performed trajectory surface hopping (TSH) simulations of nonadiabatic molecular dynamics (NAMD) of DMABN using the SHARC NAMD package, showing the capacity of our method to simulate NAMD problems. We performed timing and memory scaling tests, demonstrating that the gradient calculations of both $GW$ and BSE scale asymptotically as $N^4$ for timing and $N^3$ for memory. All source codes used in the present work, including the REST program package and the modified SHARC NAMD package that supports REST as the quantum chemistry backend, are available on Gitee https://gitee.com/restgroup/rest and https://gitee.com/qirui-gao-2025/sharc
