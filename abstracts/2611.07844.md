# Half-integer quantum anomalous Hall effect in MnBi$_2$Te$_4$ via non-magnetic extension

**Authors:** Igor P. Rusinov, Tatiana V. Menshchikova, Evgueni V. Chulkov

Within an ab-initio approach, we study the electronic structure and Hall response of the antiferromagnetic topological insulator film of MnBi$_2$Te$_4$ in contact with non-magnetic GeBi$_2$Te$_4$ (Bi$_2$Te$_3$) overlayer. The proposed junction design eliminates the exchange splitting one of the Dirac cones of the MnBi$_2$Te$_4$ (0001) thus manifesting a half-integer quantum anomalous Hall phase. The described recipe allows to enlarge temperature of the effect observation, enriches the diversity of topological properties of the MnBi$_2$Te$_4$ compound and is appropriate for other layered antiferromagnetic topological insulators. Applying the proposed heterostructure design, the half-quantized Hall phase can be established with employing other overlayer and magnetic topological insulator films.
