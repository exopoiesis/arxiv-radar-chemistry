# Holographic Butterfly Velocity and Heavy-Quark Drag in a Doubly Anisotropic Magnetized QGP

**Authors:** Le Zhang, Xiao-Wei Zhang, Guo-Dong Zhou

We investigate butterfly velocity and heavy-quark energy loss in a strongly coupled quark-gluon plasma with two distinct sources of anisotropy, generated by collision geometry and an external magnetic field, within a five-dimensional holographic model exhibiting magnetic catalysis. In contrast to studies restricted to isotropic or singly anisotropic media, we analyze the butterfly velocity and heavy-quark drag force along all three orthogonal spatial directions at finite temperature and chemical potential in the deconfined phase. We find that the collision-induced anisotropy plays a dominant role in the directional dependence of both butterfly velocity and heavy-quark drag. It governs the directional splitting of the butterfly velocity, and significantly enhances the parallel drag force while suppressing the transverse components. For butterfly velocity, a strong magnetic field primarily suppresses the longitudinal butterfly velocity while leaving the transverse components nearly unchanged. For the drag force, the magnetic field breaks the transverse symmetry, yet it does not alter the overall monotonic increase of the drag force with heavy-quark velocity, temperature, and chemical potential. Most importantly, by bridging the Einstein and string frames via the Weyl transformation, we extract the frame-dependent drag-chaos relation. Generalizing it to the doubly anisotropic plasma, we demonstrate that despite the underlying frame-dependent chaos, the normalized directional drag exhibits a direction-dependent geometric correction at order $\mathcal{O}(v^{3})$. These results establish a direction-resolved connection between heavy-quark dissipation, information scrambling, and the underlying anisotropic geometry of magnetized QGP.
