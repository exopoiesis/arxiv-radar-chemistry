# Spin-Wave Excitations in $\mathrm{La_3Ni_2O_7}$: A Dynamical Mean-Field Theory and Bethe-Salpeter Equation Study

**Authors:** Zhihui Luo, Zhong-Yi Xie, Cui-Qun Chen, Wéi Wú, Dao-Xin Yao

We perform a dynamical mean-field theory (DMFT) + Bethe-Salpeter equation (BSE) study of collective spin excitations in $\mathrm{La_3Ni_2O_7}$, using a bilayer two-orbital Kanamori-Hubbard model derived from density functional theory. Our DMFT calculations reveal a double spin-stripe ground state. On this basis, we apply the BSE to compute the lattice transverse spin susceptibility, which exhibits a damped spin-wave spectrum with a bandwidth of $\sim$0.1 eV and a wave vector of ($π$/2, $π$/2), in good agreement with resonant inelastic x-ray scattering and inelastic neutron scattering data. We inspect the orbital-resolved bare susceptibility that provides insight into the momentum dependence of the spin-wave line shape. Further analysis of the irreducible vertex reveals notable frequency dependence in the $d_{z^2}$ orbital. Finally, we investigate the evolution of the spectrum with varying Hund's coupling and electron fillings. Our study bridges the localized and itinerant descriptions of spin excitations in $\mathrm{La_3Ni_2O_7}$.
