Interfacial Structure and Dynamics of Active-Passive Fluid Mixtures

Linnea Heitmeier

arXiv:2611.16973·cond-mat.soft·Published 2026-11-12

We investigate the interface between an active and a passive fluid using molecular dynamics simulations of active Brownian particles in contact with a passive Lennard-Jones fluid. We compute the surface tension via two independent routes, pressure anisotropy and capillary-wave analysis, and find that they disagree systematically once activity is switched on. Static density and orientation profiles reveal three distinct activity regimes, with particles preferentially oriented toward the interface. Going beyond these static properties, we characterize the interface dynamics: a threshold activity above which mixing becomes irreversible, coinciding with the onset of the mixing regime, and a transient overshoot in interfacial width before relaxation to steady state.
