# The transverse spin-gradient approximation in noncollinear spin density functional theory

**Authors:** Mihály A. Csirik

We prove a quantitative error bound for a fixed-axis variant of the transverse spin-gradient approximation (TSGA) in noncollinear spin density functional theory. Inspired by Eich and Gross, we use a spin-spiral wave (SSW) as the local reference for the grand-canonical Levy-Lieb energy with the Hartree term subtracted. We construct the thermodynamic limit of the electron gas with constant total density and a prescribed SSW magnetization. Its energy per unit volume $e_{\rm SSW}$ is evaluated at the local wavevector $\nabla\varphi$, where $\varphi$ is the spiral phase. When $\varphi$ is the azimuthal angle of the magnetization $\mathbf{m}=(m_1,m_2,m_3)$, the approximation reads \[   E(ρ,\mathbf{m})\approx\int_{\mathbb{R}^3}e_{\rm SSW}\big(ρ(\mathbf{x}),\mathbf{m}(\mathbf{x}),\nabla\varphi(\mathbf{x})\big)\,d \mathbf{x}. \] Here $\nabla\varphi$ measures the local rotation of $\mathbf{m}$ about the third axis. Wherever $m_1^2+m_2^2>0$, it equals $(m_1\nabla m_2-m_2\nabla m_1)/(m_1^2+m_2^2)$. The approximation thus depends only on the total density $ρ$, the magnetization and its first derivatives. When the spin-polarized density in the rotating frame and the local wavevector vary on the length scale $N^{1/3}$, with $N$ the mean number of particles, we obtain an error $O(N^{5/6})$ for an energy of order $N$. Setting the phase to zero gives the noncollinear local spin density approximation (LSDA) for arbitrary magnetization directions.
