Total kinetic energy of fission fragments within time-dependent density functional theory: role of particle-number projection and collective deformations

B. Li, D. Vretenar, T. Nikšić, P. W. Zhao, J. Meng

arXiv:2611.32599·nucl-th·Published 2026-11-24

Time-dependent density functional theory (TDDFT) calculations of fission fragment total kinetic energy (TKE), restricted to axially symmetric trajectories and a single symmetry-violating mean-field state, underestimate TKE by 10-20 MeV away from the most probable mass split. We reexamine this for $^{240}$Pu and $^{252}$Cf within relativistic TDDFT+BCS, combining particle-number projection (PNP) with an extended collective space including hexadecapole and non-axial deformations. Varying the pairing strength leaves the TKE essentially unchanged, but PNP converts each trajectory into a resolved TKE distribution over fragment mass, and the enlarged collective space is needed to reach the correct scission configurations. Together, these reproduce the empirical TKE mass dependence substantially better than standard calculations, indicating the discrepancy reflects missing symmetry restoration and configuration space rather than a deficient energy functional.
