Tunable Long-Range Magnetic Exchange in Adatom-Decorated Transition Metal Dichalcogenide Monolayers: An ab initio Embedding Model Study

Xiaohang Jia, Ryan Weiss, Yusong Bai, Brenda Rubenstein

arXiv:2611.16085·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-11

Engineering magnetic interactions in two-dimensional materials offers a promising route toward programmable magnetic phases and reconfigurable spintronic functionalities. Here, we investigate magnetic adatoms on metallic monolayer transition-metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) as a platform for tunable indirect exchange, focusing on Fe adatoms on monolayer TaS$_2$. In the intermediate-distance regime, DFT+U calculations reveal atomic-scale oscillations in the exchange coupling between two Fe adatoms and show that biaxial strain tunes the coupling. We develop a first-principles embedding model that reproduces the DFT-calculated pair exchange and establishes a substantial RKKY contribution to the exchange; controlled comparisons further identify RKKY-mediated coupling as the primary origin of the exchange's strain tunability. These results point to combining adatom arrangement with strain as a means of engineering reconfigurable magnetic interactions in metallic monolayer TMDs.
