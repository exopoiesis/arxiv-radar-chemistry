# Kitaev Exchange and Altermagnetism in the Double Perovskite Sr$_2$CoTeO$_6$: An Ab Initio Study

**Authors:** Alexey V. Ushakov, Sergey V. Strelsov

The Co$^{2+}$-based double perovskite Sr$_2$CoTeO$_6$ attracts attention due to its nontrivial magnetic properties. \textit{Ab initio} density functional theory calculations reveal a substantial ferromagnetic Kitaev exchange $|K|\sim J/3$ between nearest neighbors that, combined with the face-centered cubic lattice, leads to pronounced exchange frustration and places Sr$_2$CoTeO$_6$ among Kitaev materials with moderate Kitaev interaction strength. In addition, Sr$_2$CoTeO$_6$ is found to be altermagnetic with a sizable non-relativistic spin splitting, which can give rise to a transverse piezomagnetic effect and a nontrivial magneto-optical response.
