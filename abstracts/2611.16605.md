# Dynamics and Statistical Mechanics of Born-Oppenheimer Auxiliary Velocities

**Authors:** Paula Sierra, Benjamin Rotenberg, Sara Bonella

In Born-Oppenheimer (BO) dynamics, auxiliary variables such as electron densities, induced polarization and electrode charges follow stationarity conditions, yet their time derivatives, such as currents, are physical observables. Using Mass-Zero constrained dynamics, we interpret these derivatives as explicit dynamical variables and derive the invariant measure giving their equilibrium statistical properties. We illustrate the framework in constant-potential molecular dynamics, where the electrode-charge velocities reproduce BO current fluctuations and the frequency-dependent admittance.
