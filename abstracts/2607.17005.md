# Provably Good Prim-Dijkstra Revisited: New Theory and a Practical Algorithm for a Classical VLSI Routing Problem with LLMs

**Authors:** Keren Zhu

Large language models may make precise but dormant algorithmic problems practical to revisit, and may expose new paths toward fundamental ones. We demonstrate this possibility through Prim-Dijkstra routing, a classic VLSI problem whose terminal-only Manhattan complexity remained open despite decades of practical work. We prove weak NP-completeness, derive a continuous cost-radius tradeoff with a balanced (2,2) guarantee, and build HP-RCRST, a height-partition-based multi-mode solver. On 28 development instances, its stronger modes Pareto-dominate the published-method union on 23 and tie on five. The case shows how conflicting conjectures, counterexamples, formal checks, and implementation can reopen neglected questions. Code and reproducibility materials are available at [https://github.com/CODA-Team/hp-rcrst](https://github.com/CODA-Team/hp-rcrst).
