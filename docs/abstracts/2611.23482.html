Formal Adequacy Index (based on Polish-language material)

Paweł Patreucha

arXiv:2611.23482·cs.CL·Published 2026-11-17

Linguistic formality is one of the dimensions of stylistic variation in speech; however, simply measuring its level does not allow us to determine whether the chosen mode of communication is appropriate in a given situation. The aim of this article is to present the concept of a formal adequacy index (based on Polish-language material), which enables a quantitative assessment of how well the formality of a text matches the expectations arising from the communicative context. The proposed approach is based on the separate modelling of observed formality, determined on the basis of the linguistic features of the text, and expected formality, determined on the basis of the characteristics of the communicative situation. Adequacy is assessed by comparing these two measures, taking into account contextual tolerance thresholds, which may differ for a lack of formality and an excess of formality. The potential applications of the index are illustrated by an analysis of Polish-language emails containing apologies for being late, written by human language users or generated by a large language model. The implementation presented here is preliminary in nature and requires further calibration and empirical validation. The main contribution of this article is the proposal of a general methodological framework for the quantitative study of formal adequacy in various communicative contexts.
