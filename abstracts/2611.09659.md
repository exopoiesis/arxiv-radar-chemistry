# Quantum Integration Software Suite For HPC-Quantum Computing Workflows

**Authors:** Mateusz Meller, Joseph C. R. Thacker, Thomas W. Keal, Vendel Szeremi

In this work, we focus on addressing challenges related to modelling the integration of conventional high-performance computing (HPC) and quantum computing (QC). We introduce the Quantum Assembly Compiler (QASC) for OpenQASM3.0 files. The compiler produces an executable that leverages the high-performance simulator QuEST to simulate quantum programs. Additionally, we introduce a performance modelling tool, QACC-perf, which can estimate the cost of integrating QC in classical HPC workflows, as well as the execution time of the quantum circuits. Finally, we demonstrate the utility of these tools in the context of multiscale quantum chemistry workflows using the ChemShell computational chemistry environment.
