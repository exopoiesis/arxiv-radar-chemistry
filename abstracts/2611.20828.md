# LAMOST III Low-resolution Spectroscopic Survey: Overview and Target Selection

**Authors:** Shuai Xu, Haibo Yuan, Maosheng Xiang, Bingqiu Chen, Yang Huang, Chun Wang, Hailong Yuan, Tianqi Cang, Xuefei Chen, Yuqin Chen, Yao-Quan Chu, Wenyuan Cui, Subo Dong, Min Fang, Shuai Feng, Jianning Fu, Wei-Min Gu, Yan-Xin Guo, Zhanwen Han, Jinliang Hou, Wen Hou, Bowen Huang, Zhiying Huo, Xiao Kong, Xu Kong, Guangwei Li, Haining Li, Jiangdan Li, Jiao Li, Chao Liu, Jifeng Liu, Qichun Liu, Xiao-Wei Liu, Xue Lu, Ali Luo, Zexi Niu, Dan Qiu, Shiyin Shen, Jianrong Shi, Botian Tang, Hao Tian, Haijun Tian, Zhijia Tian, Jian-Min Wang, Jie Wang, Xiaofeng Wang, Yuanchang Wang, Hong Wu, Jianghua Wu, Xue-Bing Wu, Qiqi Xia, Kai Xiao, Jiwei Xie, Hongliang Yan, Lin Yang, Xiaohu Yang, Haotong Zhang, Huawei Zhang, Hui Zhang, Liyun Zhang, Meng Zhang, Zhi-Xiang Zhang, Gang Zhao, Gong-Bo Zhao, He Zhao, Yong-Heng Zhao, Zhenya Zheng, Jing Zhong, Weikai Zong

We present an overview of the LAMOST III Low-resolution Spectroscopic Survey (LRS), including its scientific goals, target selection strategy, and current observing status. Targets in the main survey are selected via weighted random sampling, using the inverse local density in the Hertzsprung-Russell (HR) diagram and in the G-magnitude distribution, with additional enhancement in selected regions of the HR diagram. The main survey is complemented by 35 dedicated add-on catalogs (including very metal-poor star candidates, halo stars, hot/young stars, wide binaries, extragalactic sources and so on) and a time-domain program providing multi-epoch spectroscopy of binaries and other peculiar systems. LAMOST III LRS is designed to map the structure and evolution of the Galactic disk, reconstruct the assembly and early chemical-enrichment history of the Galactic halo, improve stellar-parameter calibration, and characterize rare and variable objects. By June 2025, the LAMOST III LRS had obtained 1.53 million low-resolution spectra from 658 plates, corresponding to 1,264,052 unique sources. Relative to LAMOST I+II, the current sample is enriched in O/B stars, subgiants, main-sequence turn-off stars, metal-poor stars, and other previously underrepresented populations. In particular, the fractions of metal-poor and very metal-poor stars increase from 3.8% and 0.4% in LAMOST I+II to 9.0% and 1.1% in LAMOST III LRS, respectively, demonstrating the improved efficiency of the revised target-selection strategy for Galactic archaeology and stellar astrophysics.
