Epitaxial integration of GaN $μ$-platelets and thin films on metallic TiN using a hybrid approach

Philipp M. John, Thomas Auzelle, Pierre-Marie Coulon, Blandine Alloing, Ileana Florea, Mikel Gómez Ruiz, Jonas Lähnemann, Lutz Geelhaar, Oliver Brandt

arXiv:2611.17986·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-13

We demonstrate the synthesis of epitaxial, self-organized GaN $μ$-platelets on metallic TiN thin films. To this end, we first nucleate self-assembled GaN nanowires with number densities between 0.01 to 300 $μm^{-2}$ by molecular beam epitaxy. Subsequent overgrowth of these seed nanowires by metal-organic vapor phase epitaxy yields GaN $μ$-platelets with corresponding number densities. The platelets exhibit N-polarity and form an ohmic contact to the metallic TiN substrate. By adjusting the growth conditions during the metal-organic vapor phase epitaxy, particularly the carrier gas and precursor ratio, the morphology of the GaN can be controlled across a broad range. Growth under N$_{2}$ carrier gas and with a 1:1 NH$_{3}$/trimethylgallium precursor flow ratio results in platelet ensembles with broad diameter and height distributions. In contrast, employing an N$_{2}$/H$_{2}$ carrier gas mixture promotes the formation of more homogeneous ensembles with narrower size distributions around a diameter of $\approx$2 $μm$. Finally, N-rich growth conditions under H$_{2}$ carrier gas enhance lateral growth, leading to the formation of coalesced GaN layers. GaN grown under these latter conditions exhibits strong near-band-edge emission, accompanied by only weak deep-level luminescence at room temperature.
