# Catalytic compression of classical communication in exact entanglement transformations

**Authors:** Vito Viesti, Alexander Streltsov

Entanglement catalysis is usually associated with making otherwise impossible transformations possible. Here we show that it can also change how much classical communication a transformation requires. A catalyst, returned exactly and uncorrelated with the output, can reduce the message alphabet size required for exact transformations of bipartite pure states by a factor that grows without bound as the Schmidt rank increases. In the asymptotic many-copy regime, however, this advantage disappears at the level of the leading communication rate: the optimal catalytic and noncatalytic rates coincide. We characterize the optimal catalytic cost in terms of Rényi entropies, revealing how the entanglement structure of the source and target governs the finite-copy advantage. Our results establish reusable entanglement as a resource for compressing classical communication in quantum state manipulation.
