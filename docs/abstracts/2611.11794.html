ChemInsight: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Evaluating and Diagnosing Chemistry Understanding and Reasoning in Large Language Models

Changyu Hu, Yiming Zou, Xin Tang, Yiru Pan, Zicheng Huang, Deng Zhao, Qing Cui, Zhenduo Zhang, Wang Ren, Xiao Li

arXiv:2611.11794·cs.CL·Published 2026-11-09

We introduce ChemInsight, a benchmark designed to evaluate large language models (LLMs) on chemistry tasks that require multi-step reasoning and structured chemical understanding. We propose a two-dimensional scoring system (scope and depth) to assess both task coverage and reasoning completeness. ChemInsight consists of 203 challenging chemistry questions organized into 50 bundles, which support the two-dimensional scoring evaluation scheme. We evaluate ten representative LLMs using ChemInsight and observe consistently low performance across models, with scope scores below 30% and depth scores below 11%, substantially lower than human expert performance. We then proceed to ask (1) What factor limits the advancement of current LLMs in chemistry? (2) To what extent are statistically driven language-model training methods effective at modeling chemistry symbolic language such as SMILES? And (3) Can LLM performance in chemistry be improved through intensive training on large, domain-specific corpora?
