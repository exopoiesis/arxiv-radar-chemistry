Altermagnetism of MnSiN2 and MnGeN2 in a wurtzite-based crystal structure

Ilteris K. Turan, Jonathan Agrinya, Walter R. L. Lambrecht

arXiv:2611.30865·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-23

Based on symmetry analysis, the Pna2_1 wurtzite-based crystal structure with Mn as a magnetic group-II element, MnSiN2 and MnGeN2 belong to the class of altermagnets because spin-up and spin-down Mn in the unit cell are related by rotation or mirror plane type operations rather than inversion or translation. Spin polarized density functional theory calculations and quasiparticle self-consistent (QS) GW calculations support the symmetry analysis and identify the lowest-energy spin configuration as Pna'2_1' in agreement with neutron diffraction data. The altermagnetic band splittings allowed for the three different antiferromagnetic ordering patterns are reported and symmetry analyzed. Additionally, spin wave dispersions, chiral magnon spltting, and Neel temperature estimates from the mean-field and random-phase approximations are provided, as well as calculations of the optical dielectric function using the Bethe-Salpeter Equation method. MnSiN2 exhibits both Wannier-type excitons with N-p to Si-s transition character and charge-transfer Frenkel-type excitons with hole on N-p and electron on Mn-d orbital character.
