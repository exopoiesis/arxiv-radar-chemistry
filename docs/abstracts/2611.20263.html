Self-doped correlated adatom lattices on cubic boron nitride

Niklas Enderlein, Tim Kullick, Henri Menke, Roland Gillen, Janina Maultzsch, Philipp Hansmann

arXiv:2611.20263·cond-mat.str-el·Published 2026-11-15

We survey triangular adatom lattices on the (111) surfaces of cubic boron nitride (cBN) for strongly correlated two-dimensional electron systems. Using density functional theory combined with the constrained random phase approximation and charge self-consistent dynamical mean-field theory, we identify twelve promising candidates. Group-III (group-V) adatoms on the N- (B-)termination realize clean one-band Hubbard systems, while group-III and transition-metal (V, Cr, Mn) adatoms on the B-termination realize two-band Hubbard systems. Importantly, the polar III-V character of cBN induces self-doping by $\pm 1/4$ electron away from integer filling. With strong Hubbard (and Hund's) interactions and small hoppings, most systems are doped Mott insulators. Heavy quasielectrons with mass renormalizations up to 4 and strong local moments up to 2.5 $μ_B$ put our systems into a regime where unconventional superconductivity on the triangular lattice has been predicted.
