# A unified reduced-mass parametrization of effective chromoelectric interactions in hadrons

**Authors:** Wen-Nian Liu, Duo-Jie Jia, Fu-Quan Dou

We construct a unified effective mass relation in which a common reduced-mass function describes the flavor dependence of chromoelectric pair contributions, with explicit color factors connecting $q\bar q$ and $qq$ pairs. After subtracting fixed spin-weighted chromomagnetic contributions, we jointly fit fourteen spin-averaged meson and baryon masses using constituent quark masses and an additive constant shared by both sectors, obtaining a root-mean-square (RMS) residual of 4 MeV. The fitted saturating form connects the nearly saturated light-flavor contribution with increasing attraction in the heavy-flavor region. In the common saturation limit, the leading chromoelectric contribution acts as a common mass shift for each constituent and can be absorbed into effective constituent masses; the residual pair term accounts for the flavor dependence away from saturation. With the chromomagnetic interaction included, this single parameter set reproduces the ground-state meson and baryon spectra and yields predictions for multiply heavy baryons outside the calibration set. By diagonalizing the sum of the chromoelectric and chromomagnetic mass matrices, we further obtain the ground-state spectra of $cc\bar c\bar c$, $bb\bar b\bar b$, and $bc\bar b\bar c$. The fully charmed tensor mass is ${6505}{(9)(9)}$ MeV, close to recent lattice QCD results, while the upper scalar mass of {${6562}{(9)(12)}$} MeV lies in the mass region near $X(6600)$. The tetraquark masses remain relatively stable under the examined variations of the $qq$ chromomagnetic couplings. The relative $S$-wave rearrangement-decay weights obtained from color--spin recoupling can be used to identify favored decay channels under the stated spatial-overlap approximation.
